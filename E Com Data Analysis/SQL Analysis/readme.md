# **End-to-End E-commerce Business Analysis Using SQL**

## Project Concept

This project analyzes a complete e-commerce business dataset using **PostgreSQL**. The purpose of the project is to understand how an online business is performing across sales, customers, products, marketing, returns, payments, inventory, reviews, and website funnel activity.

The project is designed to show how SQL can be used not only to query data, but also to solve real business problems. The analysis connects 10 relational tables and converts raw transactional data into business insights that can support decision-making.

## Dataset Overview

The dataset contains 10 e-commerce tables:

```text
customers
products
orders
order_items
payments
returns
marketing_campaigns
sessions
inventory
reviews
```

These tables represent the main areas of an online retail business, including customer profiles, product details, order transactions, payment records, return reasons, campaign results, website sessions, stock movement, and customer reviews.

## Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to answer important business questions such as:

* How much revenue and profit is the business generating?

## Executive Summary

The business generated strong overall performance with more than **$4.48M in total revenue** and **$1.46M in total profit**. The profit margin is healthy at around **32.65%**, which shows that the business is profitable.

However, the analysis also found some areas that need attention. The return rate is around **7.35%**, and damaged products, late delivery, and quality issues are the main causes of returns. Electronics has the highest return rate among product categories, so it should be reviewed carefully.

Mobile Accessories and Audio are the strongest revenue-generating sub-categories. On the other hand, Beauty, Hair Care, and Kitchen show strong profit margins, meaning they are important for profitability.

The marketing analysis shows that the Ramadan Offer campaign performed best, while several other campaigns had weak ROAS. Funnel analysis shows that mobile traffic converts better than desktop and app traffic, especially from Google, Instagram, and YouTube.

Customer analysis shows that premium and high-value customers play an important role in revenue generation. The project also uses RFM segmentation and cohort analysis to identify customer retention opportunities.

## Key Insights

* The business is profitable with a strong profit margin.
* A small group of products generates most of the revenue.
* Mobile Accessories and Audio drive high revenue.
* Beauty and Hair Care generate stronger profit margins.
* Ramadan Offer is the best-performing marketing campaign.
* Mobile users convert better than desktop and app users.
* Electronics has the highest product return rate.
* Damaged products are the biggest return reason.
* Some high-revenue products also have refund and quality risk.
* Customer retention needs improvement after the first purchase.
* Inventory is currently healthy, but high-turnover products should be monitored.

## Business Recommendations

1. Focus marketing and inventory planning on high-value A-category products.
2. Promote high-margin categories such as Beauty, Hair Care, and Kitchen.
3. Scale successful campaigns like Ramadan Offer.
4. Redesign low-performing campaigns before increasing ad spend.
5. Improve product packaging and supplier quality control to reduce damaged returns.
6. Review Electronics products because this category has the highest return rate.
7. Improve app and desktop checkout experience to reduce cart abandonment.
8. Use RFM segmentation to target Champions, Loyal Customers, At Risk Customers, and Lost Customers.
9. Launch retention campaigns within 1–3 months after a customer’s first purchase.
10. Monitor high-turnover products and prepare early restocking plans.
